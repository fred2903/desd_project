\section*{Timing Analysis}

The implemented design demonstrates the timing analysis yields compliant results for both setup and hold constraints at the target operating frequency of 151~MHz.

Regarding the setup time, the most critical path is located within the \texttt{moving\_average\_0} block (Path 21). While the design requirement demands a 151~MHz clock, this implementation achieves a positive slack of 0.230~ns. This safety margin allows the circuit to potentially operate at a maximum frequency of approximately 155~MHz.

Concerning the hold time, the critical path is located within the \texttt{axis\_fifo\_0} (Path 31). The required hold time constraint is 0~ns, and the implementation guarantees a safe positive hold slack of 0.049~ns, ensuring that no race conditions occur.

The detailed parameters resulting from the implementation are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:setup_report} for setup time and in Table~\ref{tab:hold_report} for hold time below, related to the above mentioned most critical paths.
